- Reviewers can now mark each claim **checked** or **disputed** with a comment on the review screen's Claims tab (`PUT /api/runs/{id}/marks/{claim_id}`, `GET /api/runs/{id}/marks`); the header shows "N of M claims checked" before Accept, every mark is kept with who made it and when in the run's `trace.json` (`review_marks`), and marks are final once the report is accepted. A return hands each disputed claim and its comment to the revision's writer beside the note (with a disputed claim the note may be left empty). Two opt-in profile settings, both off by default: `format.production_statement: true` ends the Markdown, HTML and Word renders with "How this report was produced" (the models each role used, the standards pack and its hash, the audit result and "N of M claims checked by <reviewer>"), and `review.require_key_judgments_checked: true` makes Accept wait until every claim a key judgment rests on is checked and none is disputed.
